The cast

The same Jesus and John the Baptist as in John, so both books share one cast. Mark adds two: Simon Peter, who is never far from Jesus in this gospel, and Mary Magdalene, who watches the cross, the burial and the empty tomb.

Scripture from the King James Version, which is in the public domain.
