The cast

2 Chronicles follows Solomon's temple and the kings of Judah to the exile. Solomon, Hezekiah and Josiah keep their faces from Kings. Rehoboam's portrait was made from his father's, and Asa and Jehoshaphat are new.

Scripture from the King James Version, which is in the public domain.

Every image was generated locally on a Ryzen AI Halo workstation with FLUX.2 [klein] and Qwen-Image-Edit, using reference portraits to keep the characters consistent from scene to scene. They are imaginative interpretations, not historical records.
